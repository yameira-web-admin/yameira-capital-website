]]> By: Yameira Capital ../index.html#comment-309282 Thu, 21 Aug 2025 13:31:53 +0000 https://ibkrcampus.com/trading-lessons/trading-outside-regular-trading-hours-rth/#comment-309282 In reply to Surya.

Thank you for your question about your Iron Condor order outside of Regular Trading Hours (RTH).
The message you received is actually confirming that your order will be sent to the exchange at 8:30 AM CT, which is when Regular Trading Hours begin. This is happening because:
You submitted your order during non-regular hours
Your order has “Enable RTH” selected (which is the default setting)
Complex option orders like Iron Condors can typically only be executed during Regular Trading Hours
When you receive the message that “the order will be sent to exchange at 8:30 AM CT,” this means:
Your order is accepted by YMC’s system
It will be queued until the market opens
At 8:30 AM CT, it will be routed to the exchange for potential execution
You’re not missing anything – this is the expected behavior for RTH-enabled option orders submitted outside of market hours. The order isn’t being rejected; it’s simply being held until regular market hours begin.
If you want to modify any aspects of your order before it’s sent to the exchange, you can do so through the Pending Orders section of your trading platform. We hope this helps!
