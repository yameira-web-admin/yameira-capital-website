Today's Snapshot

DAILY REPORT

Corn

Settlement price, volume, and OI for the Dec '26 Corn contract all saw increases Wednesday. Settlement price climbed 13'0 (+2.48%) to 536'4. The trading range was significantly bigger than even that large jump, having traded between a low of 521'2 and a high of 538'6.

The Nov '26 Soybeans contract's settlement price was also up, but not as much, increasing 28'2 (+2.28%) to 1266'0. That caused the Nov '26 Soybean/Dec '26 Corn ratio to drop from 2.364 to 2.360. This is the lowest the ratio has been in the past three months.

Implied volatility for the Dec '26 option expiry rose significantly, increasing from 23.72% to 25.42%. That puts it 3.03 pts higher than it was 90 days ago. 25 delta risk reversal

The 25 Delta risk reversal is the difference between the 25 Delta Call implied volatility and the 25 Delta Put implied volatility, multiplied by 100 for readability.

For example, in this report, the 25 Call Delta has an implied volatility of 0.28, while the 25 Put Delta has an implied volatility of 0.24.

The calculation is therefore: (0.28 - 0.24) x 100 = 3.93 (the risk reversal)
