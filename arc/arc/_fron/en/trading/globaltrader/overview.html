How US Overnight Trading Works

Hours

  • Trade eligible US stocks and ETFs from 8:00 p.m. to 3:50 a.m. ET.
  • Orders route to the overnight session only during these hours.

Eligible Orders

  • Limit orders only.
  • Time-in-force is Overnight.
  • Orders do not carry into the regular session.

Pricing and Execution

  • Quotes reflect the overnight session, not the regular market.
  • Execution depends on available overnight liquidity.

Risk and Movement

  • Prices can move quickly when news breaks.
  • Spreads may be wider and volume lower than during the day.

Settlement

  • Trades settle on the standard timeline for US equities.
