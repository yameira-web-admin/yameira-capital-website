]]> By: Yameira Capital ../index.html#comment-332419 Tue, 02 Dec 2025 16:22:34 +0000 https://ibkrcampus.com/?post_type=trading-lessons&p=195316#comment-332419 In reply to John S..

Hello, thank you for reaching out. If the product you are trading does not trade outside of regular trading hour session (like a US Stock option) the “Allow outside RTH” option within the Order Ticket or the Time-in-Force drop down will be grayed out and you will be unable to add the attribute. Also, MKT/LMT orders for a CME future and MKT/LMT/Stop orders for an ICE/Nybot future are active throughout the 24 hour trading day (both before and after the contract’s designated RTH session) and do not require the Outside RTH attribute to be selected, so they too will be grayed out. For more information please view: https://www.yameiracapital.com/faq?id=34474091

For more information on the key considerations for trading outside Regular Trading Hours, please review: https://www.yameiracapital.com/faq?id=691836583

Hello, thank you for reaching out. In order to view data on a chart outside of regular trading hours, you must check the requisite option in Chart Parameters. In the chart, click the Edit menu option followed by Chart Parameters. In the “Chart Time Options” section, click the box that says “Show data outside of regular trading hours” and click Apply followed by OK.
